# Project Scope

## Context

This portfolio project summarises work performed in the Power Electronics Laboratory at Christian-Albrechts-Universitaet zu Kiel between June 2022 and May 2023.

## Technical contribution

The work included:

- DC/DC converter and regulated power-supply development
- Voltage-control and current-control functions
- PWM-based converter control
- PCB design support and schematic review
- Component placement, soldering and hardware rework
- Laboratory commissioning and functional verification
- Fault isolation at circuit and component level
- Documentation of test setups and observed behaviour

## Evidence included

The repository contains two photographs:

- A detailed view of a PCB labelled `3Ph 2L SiC Inv`
- A laboratory workbench showing the PCB, soldering equipment and printed schematics

These photographs support the hands-on hardware scope. They do not establish specific converter ratings, controller gains, efficiency or measured performance. Those values are therefore deliberately omitted.

The supplied project bill of materials independently identifies the board as a three-phase SiC inverter platform and confirms its power module, gate-drive, voltage/current sensing, isolated auxiliary supplies, DC-link components, protection devices and signal-conditioning circuitry. A sanitised technical summary is included in this repository; the raw purchasing workbook is not.

## Publication boundary

This is a technical portfolio, not a complete design release. It does not publish university-owned schematics, manufacturing data, firmware, the raw purchasing BOM or confidential measurement records.
